THE MAN BEHIND THE HORN

Steve Glenn — Tuba Steve — has been putting air through big brass since 1995. A Denver kid whose middle-school band just happened to need somebody on the behemoth horn, he took the trade and never looked back. The nickname stuck during his stint at the Lamont School of Music at the University of Denver, and the horn eventually carried him south — for the past decade New Orleans has been home.

These days he's one of the city's most reliable and energetic low-end men: on stage with the James Martin Band, the New Orleans Jazz Orchestra, and the brass-roots outfit Magnetic Ear, or rolling through the streets with the Jaywalkers second line parade band. The horn has taken him around the world — in July 2022 he brought home the Individual Performance Award from the Karel van Eerd Music Competition in Den Bosch, Netherlands.

"It is this positivity that drives me... keeps me enjoying what I do; keeps me dancing when I play — because if I can't dance to it, I can't expect anyone else to!"— TUBA STEVE
